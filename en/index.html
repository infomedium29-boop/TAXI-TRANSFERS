Professional taxi and transfer service on Krk Island

Welcome to our professional taxi and transfer service on Krk Island. We provide safe, comfortable and reliable passenger transport throughout the year, with a special focus on transfers to and from Rijeka Airport and the Valbiska ferry port.

Whether you are arriving for a holiday, travelling for business or need transport to a specific destination, our drivers provide punctual pickup, comfortable vehicles and professional service.

Transfers from Rijeka Airport

We specialise in airport transfers from Rijeka Airport to every location on Krk Island and beyond. We monitor your flight arrival time so that we can meet you even when the flight is delayed.

When you need a taxi to Rijeka Airport, we organise a timely pickup so you can reach your flight without stress. We also drive to hotels, apartments, campsites and private accommodation throughout the island.
