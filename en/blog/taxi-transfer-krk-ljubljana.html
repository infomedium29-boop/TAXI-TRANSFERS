Taxi Transfer from Krk to Ljubljana

Ljubljana is a frequent destination for travellers continuing from Krk toward Slovenia, Ljubljana city centre or Ljubljana Airport. A private transfer provides pre-arranged pickup on the island and direct transport to the agreed destination without unnecessary changes.

Taxi transfer from Krk to Ljubljana

A private taxi transfer between Krk Island and Ljubljana is designed for travellers who want to arrange the whole journey in advance and travel directly from the pickup point to the final address. Instead of combining several transport options, waiting at stations and arranging another ride at the destination, pickup can be organised at your accommodation on Krk and the journey can finish in Ljubljana city centre, at a hotel, business address, station, airport or another agreed location.

This option is particularly convenient for travellers with luggage, families, small groups and passengers with a fixed onward connection. Before the booking is confirmed, the pickup point, destination, date, time and number of passengers are agreed so the key details are clear in advance.

Pickup from Njivice, Malinska, Omišalj, Krk and Valbiska

The Ljubljana transfer is not limited to one place on the island. Pickup can be arranged from Njivice, Malinska, Omišalj, Krk town, Punat, Valbiska and other available locations. Guests staying in apartments, hotels or private accommodation can therefore start the trip from an agreed address without first organising an additional local ride.

For pickup in the north of the island, see our Taxi Njivice and Taxi Malinska pages. If your journey begins or ends at the ferry port, more information is available on the Valbiska transfers page.

Transfer to Ljubljana city centre or Ljubljana Airport

Ljubljana can be your final destination, a business stop, the starting point for another journey or a connection to Ljubljana Airport. For that reason, the booking request should include the full address or the exact airport or pickup location. This allows the route to be planned around your actual destination rather than only the city name.

If you are travelling to Ljubljana Airport, include your flight time and, when available, the flight number. Departure from Krk can then be planned with a suitable time margin. Journey time varies with the starting point on Krk, road conditions, season and traffic, so airport trips should always allow enough time for possible delays.

How to plan the Krk – Ljubljana journey

For a longer international transfer it is useful to send the booking request early, especially during summer, weekends and periods of heavier traffic. Include the exact pickup location, your Ljubljana address or airport, travel date, preferred departure time, number of passengers and an approximate amount of luggage.

If arrival in Ljubljana is connected to a flight, train, bus or another fixed departure, include that time as well. The pickup time on Krk can then be planned around the real travel schedule rather than only an estimated driving time.

Private transfers for individuals, families and small groups

A private transfer is organised around a specific booking and agreed route. It is useful when several people travel together, when you have suitcases or other luggage, or when you want to avoid splitting the group between connections. The number of passengers and luggage should be included in the request so the transport can be organised appropriately before confirmation.

Guests who also need local rides while staying on the island can find information about taxi services on Krk. Longer routes within Croatia and Europe are covered on our long-distance transfers and Krk – Europe transfers pages.

Ljubljana to Krk: the return direction

A transfer can also be arranged from Ljubljana to Krk Island. This is useful for guests arriving in Slovenia by plane, train or bus, or staying in Ljubljana before continuing to accommodation on Krk. Simply provide the exact pickup location in Ljubljana and the final destination on the island.

For airport pickups, send the flight number and scheduled arrival time. For a hotel, city-centre address or another location, provide the most precise pickup details possible so the meeting point is clear.

When does a private transfer make the most sense?

A private Krk – Ljubljana transfer is especially practical when you want door-to-door travel, have more luggage, travel as a group, have an early or late onward connection, or do not want to coordinate several timetables. Pickup can be matched to your accommodation on Krk and the drop-off to your actual travel plan in Ljubljana.

If you are comparing international routes, you may also find our guides to Krk – Trieste Airport and Krk – Venice Airport useful.

How to book a taxi transfer from Krk to Ljubljana

Send your pickup point on Krk, exact destination in Ljubljana, travel date, preferred time, number of passengers and luggage details. If the transfer is linked to a flight or another fixed departure, include those details as well. After availability is checked, you receive confirmation of the time and agreed price before the journey.

Use our online transfer booking form to send the request. The more precise the information, the easier it is to organise the route and pickup time clearly.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book a transfer from Njivice or Malinska to Ljubljana?

Yes. Pickup can be arranged from Njivice, Malinska and other available locations on Krk Island.

Do you provide transfers to Ljubljana Airport?

Yes. Select Ljubljana Airport as the destination and include your flight time and flight number when available.

Can I book Ljubljana to Krk as well?

Yes. The transfer can also be arranged in the opposite direction, from Ljubljana or the airport to an agreed destination on Krk.

How early should I book?

For longer international journeys it is advisable to send the request in advance, especially in high season and for transfers connected to flights or other fixed departures.

What information is needed for a booking?

Please provide pickup point, destination, date, preferred time, number of passengers and luggage details. For airport transfers, a flight number is also helpful.
