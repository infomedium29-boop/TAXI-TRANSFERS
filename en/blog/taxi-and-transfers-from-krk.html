Taxi and Transfers from Krk

From short rides between places on the island to airport, long-distance and international routes, taxi and private transfers on Krk can be arranged around your actual travel plan and a pre-agreed pickup.

Published: 30 Sep 2026Taxi & Transfers KrkTraveller guide

Taxi transport on Krk Island

Visitors often need transport between accommodation, restaurants, towns, the ferry port and the airport. When the ride is booked in advance, both pickup and destination are known before the vehicle arrives, which makes planning easier during the tourist season.

Pickup can be arranged in Njivice, Malinska, Omišalj, Krk town, Punat, Valbiska and other accessible locations. Send the exact address or property name whenever possible.

Taxi Njivice and Malinska

Njivice and Malinska are important starting points for guests needing local rides, Rijeka Airport transfers or onward travel towards Rijeka and other cities. Pickup can be arranged at hotels, apartments, villas and other accessible locations.

For details, see Taxi Njivice and Taxi Malinska.

Transfers to Rijeka Airport

Rijeka Airport is located on Krk and is a key arrival and departure point for island visitors. Transfers can be arranged between the terminal and Njivice, Malinska, Omišalj, Krk town, Punat or another agreed location.

For arrivals, send the flight number. For departures, include the flight time and the time you would like to arrive at the airport. More information is available on Rijeka Airport transfers.

Taxi and transfers from Valbiska

Valbiska ferry port is important for passengers arriving on Krk by ferry or continuing from the island. A pre-booked taxi can connect Valbiska with Njivice, Malinska, Krk town, Rijeka Airport, Rijeka or another destination.

If pickup time depends on the ferry, include the route and expected arrival. See Valbiska transfers for more details.

Long-distance transfers from Krk

Private transport can be arranged to Rijeka, Pula, Zagreb and other cities when the journey continues beyond the island. The ride starts at the agreed address on Krk and finishes at the exact location provided in the booking.

For the main routes, read Long-distance Transfers from Krk to Zagreb, Pula, Rijeka or visit Long-distance transfers.

International transfers towards Slovenia and Italy

Longer private journeys can also be booked in advance for passengers continuing abroad. Our first guides cover Venice and Trieste, including airport transfers and return journeys to Krk.

Read Taxi Transfer from Krk to Venice – Airport and Taxi Transfer from Krk to Trieste – Airport.

How to book a taxi on Krk

Enter the pickup point, destination, date, time, number of passengers and luggage in the booking form. Add flight details for airport journeys and ferry information for Valbiska pickups.

The more precise the enquiry, the easier it is to confirm availability and price before the ride. Early booking is recommended for longer routes and busy seasonal periods.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I book pickup on Krk?

Pickup can be arranged in Njivice, Malinska, Omišalj, Krk town, Punat, Valbiska and other accessible locations.

Can I book a transfer to Rijeka Airport?

Yes. Transfers can be arranged between Rijeka Airport and destinations on Krk Island.

Do you offer long-distance transfers?

Yes. You can enquire about private transport to Rijeka, Pula, Zagreb and other agreed destinations.

Are international routes available?

Yes. International routes, including transfers towards Venice and Trieste, can be arranged in advance subject to availability.

What information should I send when booking?

Send pickup point, destination, date, time, passenger count and luggage through the booking form.
