Taxi Transfer from Krk to Rijeka

Need to travel from Krk Island to Rijeka? Find out how to book a direct taxi from Njivice, Malinska, Valbiska and other locations, where you can be dropped off in Rijeka, and what to include in your request.

Published: 3 November 2026Taxi & Transfers KrkTravel guide

Private taxi transfer from Krk Island to Rijeka city

Rijeka is a useful gateway for travellers staying on Krk Island. Some visitors are travelling to a hotel or business address; others are connecting with buses, trains, ships or scheduled appointments. The important detail is not simply getting to “Rijeka”, but reaching the right address without an unexpected additional journey.

A pre-arranged private transfer allows pickup at your accommodation or another agreed point on Krk and a direct journey to your destination in Rijeka. Instead of matching several transport timetables, you can provide the collection point, destination, desired departure, passenger count and luggage details before the trip.

Pickups in Njivice, Malinska, Omišalj, Krk town and Valbiska

The starting point makes a difference. Pickup can be requested from Njivice, Malinska, Omišalj, Krk town, Punat and other served areas. This is useful when travelling with suitcases, children or a group, especially if your accommodation is some distance from a bus stop.

If you arrive via the ferry terminal at Valbiska, include the scheduled ferry arrival in your request. For the return direction, from Rijeka to Valbiska, allow sufficient time before the ferry departure. Ferry schedules and traffic can change, so the exact meeting point and timing should be arranged in advance.

Where can you be dropped off in Rijeka?

Possible destinations include Rijeka city centre and Korzo, a hotel, a company address, a hospital or medical appointment, a bus station, the railway station and the port area. Provide the exact address or the name of the terminal where possible. “Rijeka centre” is often not specific enough for a smooth drop-off.

If you are connecting to a bus, train or ship, share its departure time and the location of the relevant station or terminal. This helps determine a sensible arrival buffer rather than relying on an approximate travel time alone. Terminal locations and local arrangements can change, so confirming the exact destination is always worthwhile.

How long does the journey from Krk to Rijeka take?

Driving time varies with the departure location, the destination within Rijeka, seasonal traffic and road conditions. A ride from Njivice or Omišalj is not the same journey as one from Krk town or Valbiska. During busy summer weekends, traffic can be slower around the Krk Bridge approaches and Rijeka.

For this reason, a single guaranteed journey time would be misleading. If you need to catch a train or ferry, attend a meeting or arrive at a medical appointment, include the time by which you must reach your destination. That is more useful for planning than an assumed average driving time.

Rijeka Airport is not in Rijeka city centre

Despite its name, Rijeka Airport (RJK) is situated on Krk Island, in the Omišalj area, rather than in Rijeka city centre. Travellers sometimes accidentally book a journey to Rijeka city when they actually need the airport, or vice versa.

If your destination is the airport, use the dedicated Rijeka Airport Transfers service and include your flight details. If you need a hotel, station or another address in the city of Rijeka, request a Krk–Rijeka city transfer instead.

Rijeka to Krk: the return journey

Transfers can also be arranged in the opposite direction: Rijeka to Njivice, Malinska, Krk town or Valbiska. This is particularly helpful for visitors arriving in Rijeka by bus, train or ship who want to continue directly to their accommodation on Krk.

For a Rijeka pickup, specify the station, hotel or exact street address, expected arrival and a contact number. If you are arriving on public transport, include which service you are using and the scheduled arrival time so the meeting point can be agreed before your journey.

Private transfer or public transport?

A scheduled bus may be a good choice when its timetable and stops match your plans. A private taxi can be more convenient if you need door-to-door transport, have heavy luggage, are travelling with children, or are visiting several people at the same agreed pickup point.

When comparing costs, remember to consider transfers to and from the bus stop, waiting time and any additional local taxi needed at the other end. For a small group, request a total transfer quote and compare it with the combined public-transport cost for all passengers.

Travelling with luggage, children or a group

Please mention the number of people, large suitcases and additional items such as pushchairs when booking. If you need a child seat or have a particular accessibility request, raise it before confirming the ride so availability and the appropriate arrangement can be checked.

If passengers need collection from more than one address, include each stop in your request. That allows the route to be considered in advance rather than adding unexpected detours on the day.

What does a Krk–Rijeka taxi transfer cost?

The price of a particular transfer depends on the pickup and drop-off addresses, travel time, number of passengers and trip conditions. A journey from Njivice to one part of Rijeka is different from a journey from Valbiska to another district, so a universal fare would not be accurate.

You can check the published price list and send an enquiry with your exact route to receive the applicable price information before confirming the reservation.

How to book a taxi from Krk to Rijeka

Use the online booking enquiry form and enter your pickup address, Rijeka destination, date, preferred time, number of passengers and luggage. If you are catching a bus, train, ferry or attending an appointment, also provide your required arrival time.

Mention any return journey or additional pickup stops. For other destinations beyond Krk, see our long-distance transfers. The more precise your enquiry, the easier it is to confirm availability and organise your trip.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book a taxi from Njivice or Malinska to Rijeka?

Yes. Pickup can be requested from Njivice, Malinska and other served locations on Krk Island. Include your exact address and preferred time.

Can you take me to Rijeka bus or railway station?

Specify the relevant station or terminal and the time of your onward departure. The final pickup and drop-off details are agreed for the individual trip.

Is Rijeka Airport located in Rijeka city?

No. Rijeka Airport (RJK) is on Krk Island near Omišalj. For flights, request the dedicated Rijeka Airport transfer service.

Is a return transfer from Rijeka to Krk possible?

Yes. Send your Rijeka pickup address and destination on Krk, such as Njivice, Malinska, Krk town or Valbiska.

How do I find out the transfer price?

Check the price list and send your pickup and drop-off locations, date, passenger count and luggage information for a specific quote.

How early should I leave Krk?

It depends on your starting point, traffic and required arrival time. Include the time of any bus, train, ferry or appointment so an appropriate buffer can be planned.
