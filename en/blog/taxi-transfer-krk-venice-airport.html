Taxi Transfer from Krk to Venice – Airport

For travellers continuing from Krk towards Venice, a private taxi transfer can connect your accommodation on the island directly with the airport or another agreed location in the Venice area.

Published: 30 Sep 2026Taxi & Transfers KrkTraveller guide

Private transfer Krk – Venice

International travel with luggage is often easier when the start and end of the journey are agreed in advance. A private transfer starts at your address on Krk, without a separate journey to a bus station or another transfer after arrival.

When booking, specify the exact destination in the Venice area, especially when travelling to an airport, so the route and departure time can be planned correctly.

Krk – Venice Airport

If Venice Airport is your destination, the transfer should be planned around your flight time. For international routes, allow enough time for traffic changes and seasonal congestion.

Send the travel date, flight departure time, passenger count and luggage details. Add the flight number if available so all information is in one booking.

Pickup in Njivice, Malinska and other places on Krk

Departure can be arranged from Njivice, Malinska, Omišalj, Krk town, Punat, Valbiska and other accessible places. This means the journey can start directly at your accommodation.

For early flights, booking in advance is especially useful. Clear travel details make it easier to confirm availability and the pickup time.

What to prepare for an international transfer

Passengers are responsible for valid travel documents and requirements connected with their journey. Before departure, check your documents, flight information and the exact destination address.

If you have a lot of luggage, children’s equipment or oversized items, mention this during booking so the ride can be prepared appropriately.

Venice – Krk: transfer to the island

A transfer can also be arranged in the opposite direction, from the Venice area to Krk Island. This is useful for guests landing in Italy and continuing directly to Njivice, Malinska, Krk town or another destination.

For an arrival transfer, send your flight details or pickup location together with the final address on Krk.

How to enquire about a Krk – Venice transfer

In the booking form, enter your pickup location on Krk, the exact destination in Venice, date, preferred departure time, passenger count and luggage. After availability is checked, the time and price are confirmed.

For other longer routes, see long-distance transfers or our guide Taxi and Transfers from Krk.

Frequently asked questions

Can you pick me up in Njivice or Malinska?

Yes, pickup can be arranged in Njivice, Malinska and other accessible locations on Krk.

Can the ride go directly to the airport terminal?

Yes. Specify the exact airport and flight information when booking.

Can I book Venice to Krk as well?

Yes, the route can also be arranged in the opposite direction towards Krk Island.

What information should I send?

Send pickup point, destination, date, preferred time, number of passengers and luggage. For airport transfers, add flight details.
