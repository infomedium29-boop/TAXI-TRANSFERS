Long-distance Transfers from Krk to Zagreb, Pula, Rijeka

Planning a journey from Krk Island to Zagreb, Pula or Rijeka? A pre-booked private transfer lets you leave from an agreed address on Krk and travel directly to a city centre, hotel, station, airport or another agreed destination.

Published: 30 Sep 2026Taxi & Transfers KrkTraveller guide

Long-distance transfer from Krk without unnecessary changes

When your journey starts on the island, the simplest option is often to arrange pickup at the place where you are staying. Instead of combining local transport, buses and another ride after arrival, a private taxi transfer connects the starting point and destination in one journey.

Pickup can be arranged in Njivice, Malinska, Omišalj, Krk town, Punat, Valbiska and other accessible locations. When sending an enquiry, include the pickup point, destination, date, preferred time, number of passengers and luggage.

Taxi transfer Krk – Zagreb

Zagreb is a common destination for guests continuing from Krk to the capital, a hotel, business meeting, bus or railway station, or Zagreb Airport. A private transfer can start directly at your accommodation and finish at the address agreed in advance.

If you are travelling to the airport, departure time should be planned around your flight, season and traffic conditions. For more specific airport information, read our guide Taxi Transfer from Krk to Zagreb – Airport.

Taxi transfer Krk – Pula

Pula and the Istrian region are useful destinations for travellers continuing their holiday, flying from Pula Airport or travelling to a private address, hotel or other location. A direct ride from Krk removes the need to coordinate several departures and connections.

Pickup can be arranged at a hotel, apartment, villa, campsite or another accessible address. For larger groups, send the number of passengers and luggage details in advance so that the appropriate transport option can be confirmed.

Taxi transfer Krk – Rijeka

Rijeka is one of the most important transport hubs for visitors staying on Krk. A transfer can be arranged to Rijeka city centre, a hotel, station, port or another agreed location, especially when your journey continues from Rijeka to another destination.

For guests staying in Njivice, Malinska and the northern part of Krk, a direct taxi to Rijeka can be a practical way to travel without searching for additional local transport. For airport journeys, see our Rijeka Airport transfers page.

Where can we pick you up on Krk?

The most common pickup points include Njivice, Malinska, Omišalj, Krk town, Punat and Valbiska. If you are staying elsewhere, send the exact address and we will confirm availability.

For local routes, see Taxi Njivice, Taxi Malinska and Taxi Krk. Travellers arriving by ferry can also find dedicated information on Valbiska transfers.

How to book a long-distance transfer

Use the booking form and send your route, pickup address, destination, date, time, number of passengers and any special notes. After availability is checked, you receive confirmation and an agreed price before the journey.

For long-distance routes, booking early is recommended, especially during busy tourist periods. If your travel time changes later, send the update as early as possible so the schedule can be adjusted.

Frequently asked questions

Can I be picked up in Njivice or Malinska?

Yes. Pickup can be arranged in Njivice, Malinska and other accessible locations on Krk, subject to availability.

Do you drive directly to airports?

Yes. Include the airport, flight time and preferred arrival time at the terminal when booking.

Can a larger group book a transfer?

Yes. Please provide the number of passengers and amount of luggage so the suitable transport option can be confirmed.

How do I get a price for the route?

Send the route and travel details through the booking form. After availability is checked, you receive confirmation and an agreed price before the ride.
