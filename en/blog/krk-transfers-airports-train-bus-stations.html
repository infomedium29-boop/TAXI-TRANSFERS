Transfers from Krk to Airports, Bus and Train Stations

Catching a flight, train or bus? Arrange a direct transfer from Krk Island to your chosen terminal with a pickup time that suits your journey.

Published: 19.11.2026Taxi & Transfers KrkTravel guide

From Krk Island to your next connection

A private transfer can make travel easier when you have luggage, children or a fixed departure time. Pickups can be requested from Krk Town, Malinska, Njivice, Omišalj, Punat, Baška and other locations, subject to arrangement.

If you are leaving an apartment or hotel, arrange an exact pickup point rather than giving only the name of the town. Some properties are located in narrow streets or away from the main road, so sending an address or map pin can save confusion on departure day. For a group, mention whether everybody will be waiting at the same location. Clear pickup details are especially useful when you have luggage and a connection to catch.

Which airports can you travel to?

Rijeka Airport (RJK) is actually on Krk Island, near Omišalj. You can also request a quote for Zagreb Airport, Pula Airport or other airports in Croatia and neighbouring countries. Availability and fares depend on your route and date.

Rijeka Airport serves Krk Island and is located near Omišalj, but journeys from different parts of the island require different planning. A pickup in Baška or Punat is not the same as a pickup in Omišalj. For Zagreb, Pula or another airport, include its full name rather than only the city. An airport transfer and a ride to the city centre are different destinations. For longer routes, arranging availability in advance helps you plan around check-in and boarding.

Bus and railway stations

If you are continuing by bus or train, specify the exact station and city. For example, a transfer to a bus or railway station in Rijeka can simplify the next stage of your trip. Include your scheduled departure time.

Rijeka is a common starting point for onward bus and rail journeys. Make sure you specify which station you need, because the bus and railway terminals are separate destinations. If your next connection leaves from Zagreb, Ljubljana or another city, send the station name and the departure time shown on your ticket. Longer journeys can be discussed individually; the route, pickup time and vehicle availability all need to be confirmed before a booking is made.

Allow enough time

Journey times vary with your pickup point, traffic, season and destination. Summer congestion near the Krk Bridge can affect travel. For flights, follow your airline’s recommended airport arrival time and allow a sensible buffer for buses and trains.

Avoid choosing the pickup time solely from the estimated driving time in a map application. Summer traffic, roadworks, congestion around the Krk Bridge and approaches to major cities may affect the journey. Allow for loading luggage as well as reaching the correct terminal. Follow your airline’s recommended airport arrival time, particularly for international flights. If you are continuing by coach or train, leave enough time to locate your platform and board comfortably.

What to include in your request

Send the pickup address, exact airport or station, date, desired arrival time, passenger count and luggage details. A flight number or onward train/bus departure time is also useful. These details help confirm availability and a fare.

The more detail you include, the easier it is to check a time slot and prepare a quote. Say whether the trip is one-way or return, whether children are travelling, and whether you have large suitcases, a stroller or unusually sized equipment. If reaching the terminal by a particular time matters, state your desired arrival time rather than estimating your own departure from Krk. Prices are quoted for the specific route and date, not as universal fixed fares.

Returning to Krk

You can also request a return transfer. Share your arrival details so the pickup location and onward journey to your accommodation can be arranged in advance.

For an airport pickup, provide the flight number and expected landing time. For an arrival by bus or train, give the station name and scheduled arrival time. Mention any possibility of delays when arranging the pickup. On the return journey, send the full address of your accommodation on Krk, especially if it is outside the town centre. The meeting point, available pickup arrangements and return-trip details should be confirmed with the driver beforehand.

Travelling with luggage, children or a group

If you are travelling with several suitcases, a pushchair or sports equipment, mention that when requesting transport. Passenger numbers alone do not always show what size vehicle will be suitable. Families and groups should include the number of adults and children and ask about child seats when needed. A suitable vehicle and any special equipment depend on availability and should be agreed before the transfer.

If your group is staying in different apartments or hotels, it may be easiest to agree on one pickup point. That avoids extra driving around the island and makes timing more predictable before a flight or onward connection. If you need multiple pickups instead, include every address in your initial request so the proposed journey and price can be checked.

Common questions before booking

Can you request an early-morning or late-night transfer? Yes, you can send the required time and date, and availability can then be checked. How much does a transfer to Rijeka, Zagreb or Pula cost? There is no single answer without the pickup location, destination and travel date. Request a quote for the exact trip rather than relying on an estimated price for another route.

Can you arrange only a drop-off or also a return? Include one or both journeys in your message. If your connection involves a ferry from Valbiska, a train departure or a connecting flight, share those details too. Before confirming a booking, check that the pickup address, exact terminal, date and target arrival time are all correct.
