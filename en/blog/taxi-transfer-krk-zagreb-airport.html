Taxi Transfer from Krk to Zagreb – Airport

Travelling from Krk to Zagreb Airport? A pre-booked private taxi transfer connects your accommodation on the island directly with the airport terminal, without extra transfers or searching for transport on the way.

Published: 30 Sep 2026Taxi & Transfers KrkTraveller guide

From your address on Krk directly to the airport

When travelling for a flight, a clear departure plan is essential. A private transfer allows pickup at an agreed location on Krk and direct transport to Zagreb Airport through a single booking.

Pickup can be arranged at a hotel, apartment, villa, campsite or another accessible address. When booking, send the exact location, flight date, departure time, passenger count and luggage details.

Departure from Njivice, Malinska, Omišalj, Krk and Valbiska

Njivice, Malinska and Omišalj are frequent pickup locations for guests staying in the north and west of the island. Transfers can also be arranged from Krk town, Punat, Valbiska and other accessible locations.

For local pickup information, see Taxi Njivice, Taxi Malinska and Valbiska transfers.

How should departure time be planned around a flight?

Departure from Krk should not be based only on the usual driving time. Season, traffic, weather and the amount of time you want at the terminal should all be considered.

Include your flight number and scheduled departure time in the booking. If your airline recommends arriving earlier, include that recommendation when planning pickup.

Zagreb Airport – Krk: transfer in the opposite direction

The same service can be arranged from Zagreb Airport to Krk. After landing, you can continue directly to Njivice, Malinska, Omišalj, Krk town or another agreed destination on the island.

For arrival transfers, send the flight number and passenger details so the booking can be linked with your planned arrival.

Luggage, families and groups

For airport transfers, luggage is often as important as passenger count. Provide the number of suitcases, oversized items or special luggage before confirmation.

Families and larger groups should send the total passenger count in the first enquiry so the suitable transport option can be confirmed for everyone.

Book a Krk – Zagreb Airport transfer

Enter your pickup point on Krk, Zagreb Airport as the destination, date, flight time and number of passengers. After availability is checked, you receive confirmation and an agreed price.

If Zagreb Airport is not your destination, see our guide to long-distance transfers from Krk to Zagreb, Pula and Rijeka.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book a taxi from Malinska to Zagreb Airport?

Yes, subject to advance booking and availability.

Can pickup be arranged in Njivice or Omišalj?

Yes. Pickup can be arranged in Njivice, Omišalj and other accessible places on Krk.

When should I leave for the airport?

Plan the pickup around the flight time, traffic, season and the recommended terminal arrival time. Send your flight details when booking.

Can I also book a transfer from Zagreb Airport to Krk?

Yes. The route can also be arranged from Zagreb Airport to your destination on Krk.
